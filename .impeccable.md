## Design Context

### Users
Current and incoming Computer Science and Information Technology students at KNUST. They want to find their community, understand their programmes, discover activities, meet representatives, and quickly find departmental support.

### Brand Personality
Youthful, inventive, welcoming. The owner explicitly wants premium, cinematic storytelling with witty, funny editorial personality: a small "hehe" alongside useful information.

### Aesthetic Direction
Expressive editorial technology showcase with real CSS photography, cool off-white, near-black, purposeful electric/cornflower blue, existing Bricolage display typography, and selective angular geometry. About should feel like a mini-site within the site, with its own photographic rhythm and connected navigation. No visual references requested; original art direction is encouraged.

### Design Principles
1. Real people carry the story; use supplied CSS media rather than invented imagery.
2. Cinematic, but instantly useful: a chapter index and direct links must bypass the narrative.
3. Vary the rhythm: large photographic moments, expressive statements, and calm information layouts.
4. Humour belongs in editorial copy; factual names, dates, guidance, and claims require approval.
5. Compose mobile intentionally; preserve complete information without motion or media.
